---
question: What platforms does packetevents support?
---

PacketEvents currently ships dedicated modules for **Paper** (Bukkit/Spigot/Paper, plus forks like *Purpur* and *Folia*),
**Velocity**, **BungeeCord** (and *Waterfall*), **Fabric**, and **Sponge**.<br/>
You can pick the module matching your platform when [adding the dependency](https://docs.packetevents.com/introduction/development-setup/).
